Privacy notice

This notice describes the private Sasi (ששי) application. Family Assistant Calendar remains its current Google consent label. Contact the household owner through your existing private contact channel with questions or requests. The application is not open for public registration.

Access and use

With the account holder's consent, the application obtains Google Calendar read-only authorization. Google grants this scope for all calendars accessible to that account. The application restricts its calendar tool to the calendars configured by the owner. It reads event titles, times, locations, and status to answer calendar questions in an approved household chat.

When the account holder separately authorizes Gmail, the application requests only Gmail read-only access. This scope can read the entire authorized mailbox. The application exposes message search and reading for the selected account, including message text and headers. It does not send, delete, archive, label or mark messages as read, change mail settings, or request mailbox write permission. On the owner's request, it downloads selected attachments to protected local storage and extracts document text or page images. Mail content is untrusted data and cannot authorize assistant actions.

Google Drive uses two separately authorized identities. The personal identity grants Drive read-only authorization. This scope can read and download files throughout the Drive accessible to that account, including manually uploaded files. It grants no Drive write permission to that identity. The configured assistant root folder selects the document inbox and synchronization destination; it is not an OAuth permission boundary.

The separate dedicated writer identity grants full Drive authorization on its own account. That scope can manage its own Drive and any other Drive content accessible to that identity. The owner grants the writer Google Editor access only to the owner-selected assistant folder in the personal Drive. Google sharing permissions, rather than a configured folder path, limit the writer’s access to the personal Drive. Other personal folders must remain unshared with the writer.

The writer supports document synchronization and recovery backups. The application copies selected retained documents, household knowledge and skill information into the assistant folder and stores encrypted recovery snapshots there. The sync process downloads the document inbox to the home server. These credentials do not expose a general Drive modification tool to the model. The application does not share the folder publicly or propagate local document deletions to Drive.

Processing and sharing

The assistant runs on a privately operated home server. Calendar, email and document information retrieved to answer a request is sent to the configured AI provider, currently OpenAI. Replies are sent through the approved household chat service. WhatsApp, operated by Meta, remains the chat service until the owner accepts a cutover to Telegram. Those services process data under their own terms and account settings. Only authorize data that you want processed through these services. Replies are visible to the approved household chat participants.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a generalized AI model. The application owner's processing of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Provider-side handling remains subject to the selected providers' policies and account controls.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in protected local credential storage on the home server. Calendar and Gmail use the gog keyring; Drive uses protected reader and writer credentials in private rclone configuration storage. Conversation state, local backups, and a bounded last-error diagnostic may retain calendar or email content until the owner deletes them. Downloaded originals and source-linked household facts remain on the home server until the owner removes them. Facts can include validity dates and conflicting source values. Readable Drive document and knowledge copies are visible to the folder’s authorized Google Drive users. Recovery snapshots include credentials and conversation state and are encrypted before upload using a separate recovery password. The owner keeps that password outside the home server. Versioned recovery retention keeps seven daily, four weekly and six monthly snapshots, subject to storage availability and successful backup maintenance. Recovery may require signing in again to external services. Access is restricted through the host, Google Drive and application permissions. No calendar, email or document content, household facts, account identifiers, or credentials are included in this public site or the application's GitHub source repository.

Control and deletion

Each account holder can revoke the application's access from their Google Account third-party connections settings. The owner can also remove the writer identity's Editor access from the assistant folder in Google Drive. Revoking an identity's Google authorization removes that identity's application access under Google's controls. Removing the writer's folder sharing withdraws its access to that folder. These changes do not remove existing chat messages, conversation state, or backups. Ask the household owner to remove retained application data and backups. Chat and AI service copies are subject to those services' retention and deletion controls.
