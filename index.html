Family Assistant Calendar

A private household assistant that answers calendar questions for its owner and approved family members. It is not offered as a public service.

The current version reads a calendar selected by the owner. It does not create, change, or delete calendar events.

Google Calendar authorization uses the read-only Calendar scope. This scope can read every calendar available to the authorized Google account. The assistant's tool limits queries to one calendar configured by the owner.

Calendar details used to answer a question are processed by the configured AI provider and delivered through the configured family chat. See the privacy notice before authorizing access.
