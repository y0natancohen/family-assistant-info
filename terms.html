Use information

Sasi (ששי) is the assistant’s name. Its current Google consent label is Family Assistant Calendar.

This is a private household tool for the owner and personally approved family members. It is not available for public sign-up and charges no application subscription or usage fee. Existing third-party account limits and terms still apply.

The Google Calendar and Gmail integrations are read-only. Answers may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Check important details in the original email or calendar. The tool does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice or emergency assistance.

The personal Google identity has Drive read-only authorization. A separate dedicated writer account has full Drive authorization and Google Editor access to the owner-selected personal assistant folder. Its write access supports document synchronization and recovery backups. A configured root folder alone does not limit OAuth permission. The approved household chat remains WhatsApp until the owner accepts a cutover to Telegram.

The owner can disable the assistant. The account holder can revoke Google access at any time. See the privacy notice for data handling and deletion information.
